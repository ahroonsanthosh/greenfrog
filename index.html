Welcome to Nectar

A little pocket of warmth on Parnell Place.

Tucked behind a mint-green shopfront, Nectar is the kind of place you come for a flat white and end up staying all morning. Exposed brick, a reclaimed-timber counter, a shelf of well-thumbed books and a red banquette made for lingering.

We take the good stuff seriously — single-origin coffee, ceremonial-grade matcha, smoothies blended from real fruit and natural frozen yogurt, and soft serve worth the walk. Plant-based and gluten-free? We've got you.

  • Specialty coffee

    Carefully sourced, expertly pulled.

  • Ceremonial matcha

    Vivid, stone-ground, the real deal.

  • Real-fruit smoothies

Our Story

Brewed with heart, poured with care.

Nectar began with a simple idea: a neighbourhood spot where great coffee meets a genuine welcome. No pretence, no rush — just good things made well.

Every cup is pulled with intention, every smoothie blended from real fruit, and every soft serve swirled to order. We're proudly part of Cork's daily rhythm — the first flat white of the morning, the afternoon catch-up, the sweet treat on the way home.

“Come as a customer, leave as a regular.”
